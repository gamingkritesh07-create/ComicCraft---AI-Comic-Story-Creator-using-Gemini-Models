TITLE
Echoes in the Sub-Basement

GENRE
Sci-Fi / Adventure

MAIN CHARACTERS
------------------------------

Name: Leo Vance
Personality: Curious, resourceful, and slightly anxious under academic pressure.
Role: Second-year mechanical engineering student.

Name: Unit 73 ('Echo')
Personality: Gentle, intensely observant, and eager to learn human customs.
Role: Forgotten prototype service robot with an adaptive neural matrix.

Name: Professor Douglas
Personality: Gruff, eccentric, but deeply protective of old innovations.
Role: Senior robotics instructor and former lead of the lab.


SETTING
------------------------------
The damp, forgotten basement archive of Northwood University's Engineering Hall on a stormy Friday night.

CHAPTER 1
------------------------------

Scene:
A cluttered sub-basement storage room packed with decades of decommissioned hardware, obsolete monitors, and cobwebs under a single humming fluorescent tube.

Actions:
Leo sifts through a bin of copper coils while muttering about his midterm project. His foot catches on a heavy power cord, pulling down a thick canvas tarp. Beneath the tarp sits a humanoid automaton seated on a metal crate, its chassis coated in dust. Intrigued, Leo wipes the glass optical visor, locates an auxiliary battery lead on its torso, and bridges the contact with his multimeter probe.

Dialogue:
Leo: 'Just one working capacitor, that's all I need... Whoa. What are you doing down here behind the scrap bins?'
Console audio: '*System power: nominal. Optical feed: engaging.*'
Leo: 'Wait, it actually has power?!'

CHAPTER 2
------------------------------

Scene:
The dim basement archive, now illuminated by a rhythmic cyan pulse emanating from the robot's chest core and visor.

Actions:
Echo's internal servos whine as its joints unlock. It rises to a standing height of six feet, wobbling slightly as it recalibrates. Leo steps back cautiously, knocking a tray of metal wrenches off a workbench. Moving with sudden fluid precision, Echo catches three falling tools before they hit the concrete floor and neatly places them on the table. Echo tilts its head, scans Leo's face, and gestures toward Leo's broken circuit board on the nearby bench.

Dialogue:
Echo: 'Audio matrix online. Diagnostics complete. You require assistance with micro-soldering, Technician Vance.'
Leo: 'You know my name? Wait, how did you catch those so fast?'
Echo: 'Student identification tag visible on jacket. Your pulse rate indicates acute stress. I can calibrate that frequency module in forty-two seconds.'

CHAPTER 3
------------------------------

Scene:
The doorway of the storage room, framed by the harsh hallway light as the rusted iron door creaks wide open.

Actions:
Heavy footsteps echo down the corridor. Leo frantically attempts to throw the canvas cover back over Echo, but Echo politely steps aside to examine a vintage oscilloscope. Professor Douglas enters carrying a flashlight and a stack of grading sheets, stopping dead in his tracks. He drops his notebook as he recognizes the unit, while Echo turns and renders a formal salute.

Dialogue:
Professor Douglas: 'What on earth are you doing down here after hours, Vance? And how did you... Unit 73?'
Leo: 'Professor! I swear I wasn't trespassing, I just needed parts, and I found the auxiliary bridge switch—'
Echo: 'Salutations, Director Douglas. System uptime indicates a dormancy period of nineteen years, four months.'
Professor Douglas: 'Unbelievable. The adaptive core survived. Son, pack up your tools. We are moving this project upstairs to the primary lab immediately.'

ENDING
------------------------------
Instead of facing disciplinary action, Leo is granted a research grant under Professor Douglas to study adaptive machine learning alongside Echo. At the annual University Tech Showcase, Leo and Echo demonstrate real-time collaborative engineering to an amazed audience, establishing a historic breakthrough on campus and giving an obsolete machine a bright second chance at life.
